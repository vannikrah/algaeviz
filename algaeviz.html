How to read it
Click a PRIMARY category to highlight databases whose main purpose is the selected category. Additionally, you can select one or more SECONDARY categories.

Click any subject, database, or data product to highlight its connected network. Other connected subjects/products remain visible, but less intensely.

Click anywhere in the network or Clear all filters to return to the normal view.
